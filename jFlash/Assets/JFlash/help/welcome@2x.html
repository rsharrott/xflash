As fellow language students, we wanted to create the best flash card program for studying words and phrases in Japanese & English.

Japanese in a Flash! has over 180 sets of bilingual data, an intelligent card selector system that works, a dictionary searchable in English, Romaji or Japanese and a slick interface driven by a fast data engine!

There is a searchable dictionary with example sentences, plus a quick way to report data corrections. You can even set the difficulty level for large sets.

We have cross-referenced example sentences with readings so you can discover new words and share them with friends.

We hope you enjoy Japanese in a Flash!
