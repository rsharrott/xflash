As fellow language students, we wanted to create the best flash card program for studying words and phrases in Chinese & English.

Chinese Flash has over 150 sets of bilingual data, an intelligent card selector system that works, a dictionary searchable in English, Pinyin or Chinese (Hanzi) and a slick interface driven by a fast data engine!

There is a searchable dictionary, plus a quick way to report data corrections. You can even set the difficulty level for sets based on your study habits.

We hope you enjoy Chinese Flash!
